# Getting started

A short guide to the five things you'll actually do in here. Nothing below requires any technical knowledge — if you can use Outlook, you can use this.

---

## 1. Bring in your contacts

From **Contacts**, click **Import contacts**. Drop in whatever spreadsheet you already have — an export from your CRM, a plain Excel file, anything. You don't need to reformat it first.

The app will guess what each column is (name, email, company, and so on) and show you its guesses before doing anything. Change any guess that's wrong. Then you'll see exactly how your people will look before they're actually added — no surprises. If some of them are already in your contacts, you'll get a simple choice: update them, skip them, or keep both.

You can always go back a step if you want to double check something. Nothing is final until the last screen.

## 2. Create an event

From **Events**, click **+ New event**. Fill in what you know — you don't need everything on day one. The app will tell you what's still missing before you can start inviting people.

Then go to the **Invitees** tab and search your contacts to add whoever you want there. You'll see a running count against your capacity as you go.

## 3. Write your invitation with the Coach

The **Compose** tab is where the writing assistant lives. It already knows everything about your event, so it'll hand you a complete first draft — you're never staring at a blank page.

From there:
- Type directly into the draft to edit it by hand, or
- Tell the assistant what you want changed — "make this warmer," "shorter," "mention the succession angle" — and it'll rewrite it for you.
- Try **"Make it more compelling"** if you want it to take a pass at strengthening the draft and tell you what it changed and why.

Nothing is ever sent automatically. Every message sits as a draft until you click **Approve**.

Once your invitation is approved, click **Generate full message suite** and the Coach will write your reminder, confirmation, final details, and every other message this event needs — all in the same voice, all editable.

If you want to add a personal line to a few specific people, use **Personal touches** on the same tab. It's entirely optional — most invitations won't have one, and that's completely fine.

## 4. Build your RSVP form

The **Form** tab lets you add and reorder questions by dragging them — attendance, guest count, dietary needs, whatever you need. A sensible starting question set is already there. When it looks right, click **Publish** and you'll get a link to include in your invitation (the app already puts it in for you as part of the Coach's draft).

## 5. Send it

The **Send** tab walks you through it: pick which message, and the app will recommend a pace based on how many people you're sending to — faster for a small list, spread over a day or two for a large one, to help your invitations land in real inboxes instead of spam folders.

Before you confirm, you'll always see exactly who it's going to, how many people, and roughly how long it'll take. Once you click send, **you can close your laptop** — sending continues on our end, and you can check back on progress any time from the same tab.

---

## Where to check on things

- **Home** always shows you what needs your attention across every event — who to nudge, what's coming up.
- Each event's **Dashboard** tab shows who's coming, who's still undecided, and whether you're near capacity.
- **Responses** shows everything that's come in, plus anything that needs your eye (a response that couldn't be automatically matched to a person, for instance) with a one-click way to fix it.

## A few reassurances

- **You can't break anything by editing.** Changing a name, a note, a draft, a question — none of it does anything to anyone. The only things that ever reach another person are the ones you explicitly approve and send.
- **Nothing sends without you saying so**, and every time it will tell you exactly who and how many before you confirm.
- **Already-sent emails can't be edited** — that would be dishonest to the person who received them — but you're never stuck; the app will always offer you a way to send an update instead.

If you ever feel lost, **Settings** is where every control lives, and it's always in the same place.
